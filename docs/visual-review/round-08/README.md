## What changes through the year

- **Snow** comes down the mountains and distant ranges from the top as winter deepens, lower on the faces turned from the light. It lies as a sheet on rock and among the trees in the woods, so the landforms still read. It covers meadows and shores, lies in patches as it comes and goes, and caps the rocks once it is deep. A place that has snow keeps it as winter deepens.
- **Trees stay where they are.** Each deciduous tree turns and drops its leaves at its own time, in its own colour: birches gold, copper beeches red, the rest gold, orange or red. Bare woods are grey-brown branches over the snow. Conifers stay green and hold snow on their lit tops. Spring brings a fresh pale green.
- **Grass and flowers:** fresh green in spring, straw from late summer, and wildflowers and blossom that fade after midsummer.
- **Unchanged:** the sky, clouds and light (the palette's), the haze (the Atmosphere slider's), the water (which reflects the seasonal land), and the land itself.
- **In the studio:** a **Season** slider under Appearance, named by season ("late summer"). It goes round in a circle from the keyboard, and recipes remember it. Recipes from before seasons open at midsummer, exactly as they were.
