## Applied after the verdict (as requested, without asking)

- **The "block" was a shrub stand**, the squat woody plant from round 2, not moss. Its flat top, steep ends and ruled base read as a mossy block. Shrubland is now a rounded dome of separate clumps of different sizes (generator), and meadow grass grows up over the base of near stands (painting).
- **Moss on rocks** no longer comes in ruled patches. It forms soft cushions that follow the form: on shadowed planes, at the rock's foot and along its top. It fades out through a feathered fringe of speckles and is tufted from yellow-green to deep green.

## Recorded for the final rendering (not this gate)

Accepted limitations of the current "pencil sketch" stage. They are tracked as task 25, which must be done before the Linux MVP package (task 15):

- **Water** should show wind and current, and more ripples.
- **Rock surfaces** should be less geometric and more complex.
