## Changed since round 5 (applied without asking, as requested)

- **More variation in size and height of greenery** (your round 5 note):
  - Forest stands have their own ages, from young thickets with small crowns to old growth with big ones.
  - Taller stands cast soft shadows onto lower neighbours and catch light on their exposed edges.
  - Scattered emergent trees stand above the canopy with their own shadows.
  - In tree silhouettes, about one crown in seven is a taller emergent tree and one in seven a young one.
- **Conifers** now read as pointed trees rather than vertical streaks.
- **Light:** crowns used to be lit from the left even when the mountains were lit from the right. Now everything follows the scene's light.
- **Water reflections:** lakes mirror their mountains, far woods and shores, rippled and fading with distance, and rivers reflect their banks. Rocks standing in water throw short dark reflections.
- **Rocks:** stronger lit and shadowed planes, a crisp lit rim on the edge facing the light, a dark line on the shadow side and a contact shadow where they meet the ground.
