## What changed

- **Six kinds of plant**, each with its own outline, crowns and colors:
  - **broadleaf:** tall rounded leafy crowns, bright green
  - **conifer:** tall dark blue-green spires
  - **birch:** airy light crowns on pale trunks
  - **shrub:** squat, woody, low and wide, olive with brown wood showing
  - **flowering:** pink and white blossom
  - **copper beech:** red-purple summer leaves
- Every woodland stand is one kind, chosen from the seed. Nearby stands show their outlines (spires versus mounds) and trunks under tall canopies.
- Forested hills and valley ridges are mixed forest: a broadleaf matrix with conifer, birch, shrub, flowering and copper stands blending into it. The mix is richer nearby, and in the distance the slopes read by their light and shadow planes.
- The near shore carries up to five stands, so even the classic lakeshore scenes show several kinds of plant side by side.
