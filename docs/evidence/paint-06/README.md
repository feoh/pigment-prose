# Task 06 evidence: painting settings, edges, compositing and performance

Recorded 2026-09-27 on Linux (CachyOS, kernel 7.2.7), Vulkan, wgpu 30. The adapter is an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Ti unless noted. Spec: [docs/painting.md](../../painting.md).

## Settings side by side

Every sheet shows the same four sample seeds, one per row: 15 and 33 are the round 4 favourites (high-vantage), 0 is tower-peak and 8 is framing-ridges. The varied setting is in each cell's label in the `.txt`. Everything else stays at its default. **Each row keeps one geometry checksum** (the `checksum` column), so paint settings never moved the scene.

| Sheet | Setting | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [vary-wash.png](vary-wash.png) | wash / gouache | 0 (translucent wash), 0.25 (default), 1 (opaque gouache) |
| [vary-haze.png](vary-haze.png) | atmosphere | 0 (clear), 1 (hazy) |
| [vary-intensity.png](vary-intensity.png) | palette intensity | 0 (muted), 1 (vivid) |
| [vary-looseness.png](vary-looseness.png) | edge looseness | 0 (crisp), 1 (loose) |
| [vary-palette.png](vary-palette.png) | palette | lakeshore, golden-evening |
| [vary-marks.png](vary-marks.png) | mark scale | 0.5, 2 |

Commands: `pigment-prose contact-sheet --out vary-KEY.png --samples 15,33,0,8 --vary KEY=V1,V2 --cell 400` (wash: `--vary wash=0,0.25,1`).

## 1:1 crops

- [wash-crops.png](wash-crops.png): seed 15 at 1920 × 1080, one foreground region at wash 0, 0.25 and 1. Wash is mottled and transparent, with pooled lines where washes meet. Gouache is flat, matte and opaque, and covers the paper texture.
- [edges-4k-crops.png](edges-4k-crops.png): seed 15 at 3840 × 2160. Top row: a near bank against the river. Bottom row: a far ridge against the sky. Left column: looseness 0.4 (the default). Right column: looseness 1. Near silhouettes stay crisp. Far edges wander and soften more as looseness rises (selective edges). At this scale the conifer spires read as vertical streaks; tree drawing is task 07.
- [texture-crops.png](texture-crops.png): seed 15 at 1920 × 1080. Top row: paper grain 0, 0.3 (the default), 1. Bottom row: granulation 0, 0.3, 1. Both textures are band-limited away below about 2 px, so they do not show in 400 px cells.

Crops were cut from `contact-sheet --cell 1920|3840 --cols 1|2 --vary …` sheets with ImageMagick `-crop`. They are not generated by the application.

## Compositing and extremes (hardware suite)

[gpu-tests-linux-2026-09-27-task06.txt](../gpu-tests-linux-2026-09-27-task06.txt): 12 of 12 tests pass on the RTX 4070 Ti, including:

- `compositing_matches_the_reference_model`: 576 opaque-fill, coverage, glaze and wash cases evaluated by the painting shader's own functions against `pigment_core::composite`. Worst relative error 7.6e-8 (tolerance 1e-4).
- `painting_is_valid_at_setting_extremes`: all 128 combinations of paint-setting endpoints for both palettes (256 renders) are opaque and have no black pixels, and the scene checksum is unchanged.
- `painting_is_identical_tiled_and_single`: byte-identical at looseness 0, 0.4 and 1, with 256 and 333 px tiles.

The Intel iGPU ran the benchmark only, not the hardware suite.
